Why something always breaks

A boat sits in one of the harshest environments there is.

Sun, wind, waves and salt water work against the ship around the clock. None of it takes a break while you are away. That is why there is always something to fix on a boat.

At the same time a yacht is a highly complex piece of technology. Engine, rig, electrics, sails, safety gear, plumbing: many things have to be in good shape for you to be safe at sea. When one of them fails, you usually notice it offshore, not at the dock.

Sun

UV breaks down gelcoat, covers, sailcloth and seals. Slowly, but without pause.

Wind & waves

Constant movement in the rig, fittings and bearings. Whatever chafes will eventually give.

Salt water

Corrosion in wiring, contacts and everything metal, including where nobody looks.

Systems on board

Engine, electronics, rig and safety gear, each with its own intervals and part numbers.

Where do I find a reliable service partner nearby?

Yards, workshops, sailmakers, painters and fuel docks: thousands of providers across Europe on one map. From the Mediterranean to the Baltic, filtered by category and brand.

  • 1 Search by name, brand or service  plus the category filter for yard, engine service, rigging or sailmaker.

Where do I get the right spare part?

From any equipment entry straight to the matching spare part. Skipily sorts the hits into three clear categories instead of leaving you to hunt for the right impeller or water filter for hours.

Original parts: 1:1 matches via the part number.
1:1 derivatives: same manufacturer and model, should fit directly.
Other matching equipment: the sensible extras you need for the job.
  • 1 The search starts from your equipment. Device, manufacturer and model are already at the top. You type no part number.
  • 2 Matching equipment included. Whatever belongs to the same device is counted and expandable.
  • 3 Category, price and availability in one line. No “that'll do”, no phone call to double-check.
