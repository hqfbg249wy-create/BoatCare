Frequently asked questions

General

What is Skipily?
Skipily is the app for boat owners: find shipyards, workshops, sailmakers and accessory shops on a map, manage your own boat with equipment, keep track of maintenance and use an AI boat assistant that knows your boat.
How much does Skipily cost?
The app is free – all core features included. Optionally, there is "Skipily Plus" with unlimited AI assistant, damage photo analysis, and equipment recommendations.
What devices does Skipily run on?
Skipily is available as an app for iPhone/iPad (App Store) and for Android (Google Play). Signing in with the same account works on all devices.
In which languages is Skipily available?
In six languages: German, English, French, Italian, Spanish and Dutch. The app adapts to your device language.

Getting started

How do I add my boat?
After logging in, tap "Boats" and add your boat with make, model, dimensions, and equipment. You can store photos and serial numbers directly – everything in one place, even offline.
How do I find service providers near me?
Via the map: Skipily shows boatyards, workshops, sailmakers and shops in your area – filtered by category and distance, with ratings, route and direct contact.

AI assistant

How does the AI boat assistant work?
The assistant knows your stored equipment and answers specific questions – from "What oil does my engine need?" to maintenance steps. You can forward answers directly to a service provider.

Skipily Plus

What does Skipily Plus offer?
Plus unlocks the AI assistant without limits, enables damage photo analysis, and suggests missing equipment suited to your boat. There is a 7-day free trial.
How do I cancel Skipily Plus?
Management and cancellation is handled through your App Store or Google Play account (subscription settings). After cancellation, Plus remains active until the end of the paid period.

Account

How do I delete my account?
In the app under "Profile → Delete Account" – or by email at info@skipily.app. Your account and the associated data will then be deleted. Details: skipily.app/account-deletion.

Privacy

What happens to my data?
Your data is only used to provide the app and is not sold. Encrypted transmission, hosting in the EU. All details can be found in the privacy policy at skipily.app/datenschutz.
